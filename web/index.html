What determines the results?
Search inputs
Your location and a short description of the care needed. OpenAI suggests a care setting and search terms from the Databricks catalog. Common emergency warning phrases show immediate guidance. If a specific service is unclear, the search can start with general outpatient assessment. Databricks uses the service to retrieve and rank facilities, and resolves the search origin from the city, district, PIN or coordinates. Distances are straight-line estimates.
Dataset-only baseline
Virtue service mentions within 100 km, ordered by distance when the facility pin is usable. A mention can describe a service the campus lacks.
Needs confirmation in live searches
Explicit on-site claims come ahead of general or narrower claims, then distance breaks the tie. Reviewed source links provide context. These results are leads to confirm.
Withheld
Live searches withhold results when location checks conflict or Virtue claims conflict, indicate absence, refer elsewhere or describe a plan.
Sources used and available for follow-up

Databricks uses Virtue facility text and pins, India Post location data, and reviewed evidence where available. The external links below are available for follow-up. Searches do not run the optional crawlers or external evidence lookups.

  • Hospital directory · Public facilities and blood banks · PM-JAY · Dialysis centres · Emergency obstetric care · NABH
  • Overture, map listings, hospital websites, news, tenders and public reviews can provide leads for review. Facility listings and historical care leave current availability unknown.

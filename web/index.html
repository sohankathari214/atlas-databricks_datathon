What determines the results?
Search inputs
Your location and a short description of the care needed. OpenAI suggests a care setting and search terms from the Databricks catalog. Common emergency warning phrases show immediate guidance. If a specific service is unclear, the search can start with general outpatient assessment. Databricks uses the service to retrieve and rank facilities, and resolves the search origin from the city, district, PIN or coordinates. Distances are geographic estimates.
Service probability
Posterior log-odds equal prior log-odds plus the strongest fitted log likelihood ratio from each evidence family.
Expected time to care
Travel time is 60 × detour factor × distance ÷ speed + setup time. The ranking adds the probability-weighted cost of being turned away, travelling to the best backup and possibly still obtaining no care.
Value of a call
Current best expected time minus the probability-weighted expected time after a yes or a no, with a floor of zero. The strongest call clears the estimated call cost.
Held-out evaluation
Evaluation appears for services with enough labelled district searches.
Model assumptions
Detour factor 1.3 (1.2, 1.4, 1.6), road speed 30 km/h (20, 40), setup 10 min (0, 20), turn-away 30 min (15, 60), no-care penalty 240 min (120, 480), call cost 3 min (1, 10), prior shrinkage 10 (5, 20).
Withheld facilities
Location conflicts have no travel estimate. Negative claims and a probability below 0.10 also withhold recommendations.
Sources used and available for follow-up

Databricks uses Virtue facility text and pins, India Post location data, and reviewed evidence where available. The external links below are available for follow-up. Searches do not run the optional crawlers or external evidence lookups.

  • Hospital directory · Public facilities and blood banks · PM-JAY · Dialysis centres · Emergency obstetric care · NABH
  • Overture, map listings, hospital websites, news, tenders and public reviews can provide leads for review. Facility listings and historical care leave current availability unknown.
